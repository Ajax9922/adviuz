Part A — Digital Flyer campaigns

A1. What you are buying

  • A Digital Flyer package is a fixed number of viewed flyers. A viewed flyer is counted when a unique browser keeps your flyer open and on screen for a total of at least 25 seconds — the timer pauses whenever the flyer is not on screen.
  • Each unique browser is counted once per campaign: a person who returns to your flyer later in the same browser, or keeps it open for ten minutes, is never counted against your package again.
  • Deliveries ("sends"), ad impressions and opens shorter than 25 seconds are never charged — we deliver as many as it takes, at our cost, to reach your viewed-flyer count.
  • A lead is a person who chooses to share their contact details after seeing your flyer, delivered to your hub. You are buying delivery of viewed flyers; you are not buying leads, appointments, sales or any business outcome.

A2. How your campaign runs

  • You purchase a package, then provide your content: logo, offer, business details, and your target area and audience.
  • We design your master flyer and send it for your approval. Design and reasonable revisions before launch are included.
  • Your approval of the master flyer is your confirmation that every claim, price, offer and image in it is accurate and yours to use.
  • Most campaigns launch within 7–15 business days of receiving your complete content and approvals. This is an estimate, not a deadline: the clock pauses while we wait for your content, your approval, or an advertising platform's review.

A3. Creative variations

To get results, we create and test multiple versions of your approved flyer — different headlines, images, formats and placements — without asking you to approve each one. Every variation stays within your approved master design and brand guidelines and uses only the offers, prices and claims you approved. You can see your active creatives in your hub, and we will stop any specific variation at your request.

A4. Errors and corrections

Creatives are produced quickly and occasionally contain a typographical error. Tell us and we will correct it promptly. Minor errors do not entitle you to a refund, credit or cancellation. If an error we introduced makes your contact details, price or offer materially wrong, we will correct it and credit the affected views in good faith. Errors in information you supplied are your responsibility, though we will still fix them quickly.

A5. How views are counted, verified and protected

  • Real time, verifiable by you. Your view count updates in your hub in real time, and you can verify the counting yourself at any time: open your own flyer, keep it on screen for 25 seconds, and watch your count increase. Every viewer is measured exactly the same way — your own test views and your staff's views count like anyone else's, once per browser per campaign.
  • System of record. Views are measured by our tracking system, which is the system of record for delivery and billing. Counts in your own analytics tools may differ from ours because measurement methods differ (timers, cookies, consent settings, ad blockers); those differences are normal and do not affect billing.
  • Invalid traffic. Our system filters views it identifies as invalid (bots, automated traffic or fraudulent activity) — these are never counted against your package. We may improve our measurement and invalid-traffic technology over time; improvements never reduce views already credited to you.
  • Disputes. If you believe a count is wrong, tell us within 30 days of the views in question and we will review the data with you in good faith. Where we agree an error occurred, we credit replacement views; view credits are the sole and exclusive remedy for counting and delivery issues. Counts not disputed within 30 days are accepted.

A6. No performance guarantee

Section 14 of the General Terms applies in full: we do not guarantee leads, appointments, sales, revenue, profit or any business outcome. Our commitment is the delivery of your purchased viewed flyers.

A7. Leads

Leads are delivered to your hub as they arrive, with the consents captured at submission. Your leads are yours — we never sell or share them with other businesses. What happens after a lead arrives (speed of response, offer, closing) is in your hands, and lead volume or quality is not a ground for refund or cancellation.

  • Packages are valid for 6 months from purchase.
  • You may pause your campaign at any time; remaining views stay in your account and can be restarted, or applied to a new flyer or offer, within the validity period.
  • We send reminder emails as expiry approaches. Views unused at expiry lapse, except where the delay was caused by us — then we extend or refund the undelivered portion.

A9. Delivery pace, platforms and placement

  • Views are delivered at a pace that varies with platforms, auctions, season and your audience. We do not guarantee any daily or weekly rate of delivery — only the total within your validity period.
