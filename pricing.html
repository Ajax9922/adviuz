Pricing questions

How much does the AI Conversion Engine cost?

Plans are $600 CAD (Essential, plus a one-time $300 setup), $1,000 CAD (Pro, setup included) and $2,000 CAD (Max, setup included). Enterprise plans for multiple locations or large volume are quoted after a short call.

How much do Digital Flyer campaigns cost?

Each flyer campaign is quoted for you at a short demo, because the price depends on your audience, area, number of flyers and campaign length. You are charged only for flyers viewed for at least 25 seconds.

Is this a monthly subscription?

No. AI Conversion Engine plans are prepaid, with no contract. Turn on auto-recharge and the same plan refills when you are down to 20% of your AI minutes or 20 minutes left. You can switch it off any time.

Is there a setup fee?

Essential has a one-time setup fee of $300. Setup by our team is included with Pro and Max. Auto-recharges never include a setup fee.

Do the credits expire?

Each plan is valid for its stated period: 120 days on Essential, 180 days on Pro and 360 days on Max. Unused credits expire at the end of that period.

Can I use both products?

Yes. Many businesses run Digital Flyer campaigns and use the AI Conversion Engine to follow up with the leads. Ask about it at your demo.
