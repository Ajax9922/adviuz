Paper flyers

Why it hurtsDesigning, printing and paying people to deliver every copy costs money before a single person reads it.

Why it hurtsDoors with “No soliciting” signs, apartment buildings and gated communities are often off-limits to flyer delivery.

Why it hurtsSnow, rain and extreme cold slow delivery crews down, soak the paper and push campaigns back by days.

Why it hurtsOnce a flyer lands in a mailbox, there is no way to know whether anyone read it or threw it away.

Why it hurtsYou can't see which streets were covered or whether every flyer was actually delivered.

Why it hurtsEven when someone is interested, you rarely find out who they are unless they call you themselves.

Why it hurtsUnread paper flyers pile up in recycling bins, along with the money spent printing them.

Why it hurtsPrinting, sorting, carrying and delivering takes hours of physical work for every campaign.
