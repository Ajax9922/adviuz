From designing your flyer to helping you close the deal.

Adviuz is a unique digital flyer distribution platform and a one-stop marketing and sales solution. It covers every step, from flyer design and distribution to follow-up and help with closing the deal. Founded in 2014, headquartered in Canada, serving clients globally.

Our mission

To help businesses grow by upgrading them from paper to digital flyers, and to give them the most value from every advertising dollar.

For more than a decade, Adviuz worked mostly with large corporate brands, proving that businesses should pay only for flyers people actually read — at least 25 seconds of attention.

In 2025 the company made Canada its headquarters. Today its AI Conversion Engine follows up with every lead by call, text and email, so a single platform now covers both marketing and sales.

Founder story

We built what our clients needed

It started at his own mailbox. Ajay Sheria, an engineering graduate, saw how many paper flyers went straight into the recycling bin, and noticed that every flyer already exists as a digital file before it’s printed. In 2014 he founded Instad Web Services and launched Adviuz, a platform where businesses could upload their flyer and distribute it digitally.

From there, every step came from listening to clients:

  1. Results, without another platform to manage.Busy teams wanted outcomes, not more tools. So Adviuz became fully managed: we design the digital flyers, run the distribution and report on every verified view.
  2. Every enquiry tracked in one place.Well-targeted digital flyers brought in a large volume of enquiries. In 2016 we added a CRM, so every enquiry could be tracked from first view to closed deal.
  3. From enquiries to customers at the door.Enquiries alone were not enough, especially when a client’s sales staff changed and follow-up slipped. In 2018 we launched the Sales Support Centre. Our own team followed up with every enquiry and arranged for the customer to visit the client in person, so clients received real visits, not just enquiries.
  4. Built to run from anywhere.When Covid moved work home, we rebuilt the system as the Sales Booster app, with calling and sales tracking in one place.
  5. Follow-up that never sleeps.Today the AI Conversion Engine follows up with every enquiry by call, text and email, around the clock, giving marketing and sales one view of the pipeline from flyer to closed deal.

Along the way Adviuz worked mostly with large corporate brands. In 2025 Ajay brought the company to Canada.
