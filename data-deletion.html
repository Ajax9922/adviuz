Data Deletion

You can ask Instad Web Services Ltd., operating as Adviuz ("Adviuz", "we", "us"), Scotiabank Building, 111 2nd Ave S, Unit 400, Saskatoon, SK, Canada to delete your personal information at any time. This page explains how.

How to request deletion

  • Email support@adviuz.com with the subject line "Data deletion request", from the email address associated with your information, or
  • Call +1 647 250 1152 and ask for your data to be deleted.

What happens next

  • We verify the request came from you (we may ask you to confirm details we already hold).
  • We delete your personal information from our systems within 30 days and confirm to you when it's done.
  • We also instruct our service providers (such as Twilio and Supabase) to remove your data from their systems where it exists.

What we may need to keep

Some records are kept where the law requires it — for example, invoices and payment records for tax purposes (7 years), and a minimal record of your deletion request itself so we can prove we honoured it and keep you opted out of future contact.

Google Calendar data

If you connected Google Calendar to the Adviuz client platform, disconnecting it (from your dashboard or your Google Account permissions page) stops our access immediately. A deletion request also removes any calendar data we stored.

If you submitted your details through a client's flyer

If your contact details reached us because you responded to a business's digital flyer, we'll delete them from our systems and stop all contact from Adviuz. The business you contacted may hold its own copy; we'll tell you which business it was so you can contact them directly.
